Single source of truth for the fictional consultant used in the synthetic call scripts.

All names, numbers and contact details are fictional.

## Consultant
- Elena Ward, independent small-business consultant (trading as "Ward Advisory").
- Working hours: Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm.

## Services and pricing
| Service | Price | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery call | Free | 20 minutes, no commitment |
| Single Strategy Session | $180 | 90 minutes, written summary within 2 business days |
| Growth Package | $950 upfront, or 3 monthly payments of $330 | 6 sessions over 3 months |
| Monthly Retainer | $600 per month | 2 sessions per month + email support (reply within 1 business day), minimum 3 months |

## Process
1. Free discovery call.
2. Intake questionnaire sent within 24 hours.
3. Kickoff session within 10 days of signing.
4. Written action plan delivered within 5 business days after the kickoff session.

## Rescheduling and cancellation
- Rescheduling 48 hours or more before a session: free.
- Cancelling or rescheduling less than 48 hours before: 50% of the session fee.
- No-show: full session fee.

## Refunds
- Growth Package: full refund within 14 days of purchase if no sessions have been used.
  After that, pro-rata refund for unused sessions minus a $50 admin fee.
- Monthly Retainer: cancel with 30 days notice; the current month is not refunded.

## Payment
- Card or bank transfer. Invoices are due within 14 days.
