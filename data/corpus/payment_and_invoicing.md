# Ward Advisory — Payment and Invoicing

## Payment methods

- **Card:** every invoice contains a secure payment link. For security reasons, card details are never
  taken over the phone or by email; please use the link.
- **Bank transfer:** the bank details are printed at the bottom of every invoice. Please use the invoice
  number as the payment reference.

Cash and cheques are not accepted.

## When invoices are sent

| Service | Invoice |
|---|---|
| Single Strategy Session | When the session is booked |
| Growth Package, paid upfront | One invoice of $950 when the agreement is signed |
| Growth Package, paid in instalments | Three invoices of $330: at signing, then on the 1st of the next two months |
| Monthly Retainer | $600 on the 1st of each month; the first invoice when the agreement is signed |
| Late cancellation or no-show fee | Separately, after the session date |

## Payment terms

All invoices are due within 14 days of the invoice date.

- If an invoice is not paid 7 days after the due date, you receive a friendly reminder by email.
- If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, new sessions are paused until it is paid. Sessions that
  were already paid for are not affected.
- There are no late payment interest charges.

## Invoice copies and changes

- You can ask for a copy of any invoice by email; copies are sent within 1 business day.
- If your company name, address or tax number on an invoice is wrong, Elena issues a corrected invoice.
- Invoices can be sent to a second email address, for example your bookkeeper or accountant.

## Prices and tax

Prices are fixed for the duration of an agreement. A price change only applies to new agreements and to
retainers after at least 60 days notice. Ward Advisory's prices do not include sales tax; where sales tax
applies, it is shown separately on the invoice.

## Payment problems

If a payment failed, was charged twice or shows the wrong amount, contact Elena by phone or email.
Duplicate payments are refunded in full (see the Cancellation and Refund Policy).
