# Ward Advisory — Services and Pricing

Ward Advisory is the consulting practice of Elena Ward, an independent small-business consultant.
Elena helps owners of small businesses — shops, cafés, studios and local service companies, usually
with fewer than 20 employees — with pricing, marketing, customer retention and day-to-day operations.

All prices are in US dollars and are per client business, not per person attending.

## Overview

| Service | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Call | Free | 20 minutes, no commitment |
| Single Strategy Session | $180 | One 90-minute session and a written summary |
| Growth Package | $950 upfront, or 3 monthly payments of $330 | 6 sessions over 3 months and a written action plan |
| Monthly Retainer | $600 per month | 2 sessions per month and email support, minimum 3 months |

## Discovery Call (free)

A 20-minute call to talk about your business and the problem you want to solve. Its purpose is to find
out whether Elena is the right person to help and which service fits best. There is no commitment and no
payment information is needed. A discovery call is not a working session: it does not include advice
on your specific numbers or a written summary.

## Single Strategy Session — $180

One 90-minute working session focused on a single question, for example "How should I price my new
product line?" or "Why are my repeat customers dropping off?".

- Before the session you fill in the intake questionnaire, so the time is spent on your problem and not
  on background questions.
- Within 2 business days after the session you receive a written summary with the main findings and
  the next steps that were agreed.
- The Single Strategy Session is a good choice if you want a second opinion on one decision, or if you
  want to try working with Elena before committing to a package.

## Growth Package — $950

Six sessions over three months, for owners who want to work through a larger change step by step.

- Price: $950 paid upfront, or three monthly payments of $330 (total $990).
- Sessions: 6 sessions of 60 minutes, usually every two weeks.
- The package starts with a kickoff session. Within 5 business days after the kickoff session you
  receive a written action plan with goals, milestones and the topics of the remaining sessions.
- For late-cancellation fees, one Growth Package session is valued at $158 (one sixth of $950).
- The six sessions should be used within 4 months of the kickoff session. Sessions that have not been
  used after 4 months expire, unless a longer break was agreed in writing (for example for a planned
  shop closure or parental leave).

## Monthly Retainer — $600 per month

Ongoing support for owners who want a regular sparring partner.

- Two sessions of 60 minutes per month.
- Email support between sessions: Elena replies within 1 business day (Monday to Friday). You can send
  emails at any time, including evenings and weekends; they are answered on the next business day.
- Minimum term: 3 months. After that, the retainer continues month by month until it is cancelled with
  30 days notice (see the Cancellation and Refund Policy).
- A session that is not used in a month can be carried over to the next month only. It cannot be carried
  over a second time and it is not refunded.
- Clients who move from a Single Strategy Session to a retainer do not need to fill in the intake
  questionnaire again.

## Which service should I choose?

- One clear question, or you want to try the collaboration first: **Single Strategy Session**.
- One larger project with a clear end, such as a relaunch or a new pricing model: **Growth Package**.
- Several topics at the same time, or you want regular contact over a longer period: **Monthly Retainer**.

If you are not sure, book the free Discovery Call and decide afterwards.

## What is not included

Elena does not provide legal, tax or accounting advice, does not do bookkeeping and does not run
advertising campaigns on your behalf. When a question needs one of these specialists, she will say so
and can suggest what to ask them.

## Discounts

Ward Advisory does not offer discounts, coupon codes or special prices. Instead, the Growth Package can
be paid in three monthly instalments, and every client can start with the free Discovery Call.
