# Ward Advisory — Cancellation and Refund Policy

This policy applies to all services of Ward Advisory. Notice periods are counted from the moment Elena
receives your message by phone or email.

## Rescheduling and cancelling a session

| When you tell us | Fee |
|---|---|
| 48 hours or more before the session | Free — the session is moved or cancelled at no cost |
| Less than 48 hours before the session | 50% of the session fee |
| No-show (you do not attend and did not tell us) | 100% of the session fee |

Session fees used for this calculation:

- Single Strategy Session: $180, so a late cancellation costs $90.
- Growth Package: one session is valued at $158, so a late cancellation costs $79.
- Monthly Retainer: one session is valued at $300, so a late cancellation costs $150.

For Growth Package and retainer clients, a late cancellation fee is invoiced separately. A late-cancelled
or missed package session is not lost: it can still be rescheduled.

Exceptions: if you cancel late because of illness or an emergency, tell Elena as soon as possible. The fee
is waived once per client per year.

If Elena has to cancel or move a session, there is never a fee for you, and you can choose a new time.

## Refunds: Single Strategy Session

The $180 fee is refunded in full if you cancel 48 hours or more before the session. After the session
has taken place, it is not refundable.

## Refunds: Growth Package

- **Within 14 days of purchase, if no session has been used:** full refund of the amount paid.
- **After 14 days, or once a session has been used:** pro-rata refund for the unused sessions, minus a
  $50 admin fee.
- Late cancellation fees that are still open are deducted from the refund, so you do not need to pay
  that invoice separately.
- If you pay in three monthly instalments, any instalments that have not been invoiced yet are cancelled.

Example: you paid $950 upfront and have used 2 of the 6 sessions. The refund is
4 × $158 − $50 = $582.

## Refunds and cancellation: Monthly Retainer

- The retainer has a minimum term of 3 months.
- After the minimum term you can cancel with 30 days notice, by phone or by email.
- The current month is not refunded. Sessions you have already paid for can still be used until the end
  of the notice period.
- There is no refund for unused email support or for sessions that were not booked.

## How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to the original payment method: to the same card, or by bank transfer to the account
  the payment came from.
- Elena processes refunds within 2 business days of the cancellation and sends a refund confirmation by
  email with the amount and the date.
- A card refund usually appears on your statement within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

## Duplicate or wrong charges

If you were charged twice or charged a wrong amount, contact Elena. The extra amount is refunded in full
on the same day it is confirmed, and you receive a written refund confirmation.
