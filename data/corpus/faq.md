# Ward Advisory — Frequently Asked Questions

## Getting started

**Is the first call really free?**
Yes. The Discovery Call takes 20 minutes, is free and has no commitment. No payment details are needed.

**I am not sure which service I need. What should I do?**
Book the Discovery Call. At the end of the call Elena recommends a service, and you can ask for a written
overview by email before you decide.

**What kind of businesses do you work with?**
Small businesses, usually with fewer than 20 employees: shops, cafés, bakeries, studios, gyms and local
service companies. Elena does not work with large corporations or with start-ups that have no customers yet.

**How quickly can we start?**
The kickoff session takes place within 10 days of signing the agreement. A Single Strategy Session can
usually be booked within a week.

## Sessions

**Are sessions online or in person?**
Mostly online by video call. In-person meetings are possible for clients in the local area, at no extra
cost.

**How long is a session?**
A Single Strategy Session takes 90 minutes. Growth Package and retainer sessions take 60 minutes.

**Can I bring a business partner or an employee to the session?**
Yes, up to two other people from your business can join at no extra cost.

**Can I move a session?**
Yes. Moving a session is free if you ask at least 48 hours before it. With less notice, 50% of the
session fee is charged; if you do not show up, the full fee is charged.

**I emailed on a weekend. When will I get an answer?**
Retainer clients get email support with a reply within 1 business day, Monday to Friday. An email sent on
a Saturday or Sunday is answered on Monday.

**Do Growth Package sessions expire?**
The six sessions should be used within 4 months of the kickoff session. Longer breaks can be agreed in
writing.

## Prices and payment

**Do you offer discounts?**
No. Instead, the Growth Package can be paid in three monthly payments of $330, and every client can start
with the free Discovery Call.

**Can I pay by card?**
Yes. Every invoice has a secure payment link. Card details are never taken over the phone.

**Can I pay by bank transfer?**
Yes. The bank details are at the bottom of every invoice.

**When do I have to pay?**
Invoices are due within 14 days of the invoice date.

**Can my accountant receive the invoices?**
Yes. Invoices can be sent to a second email address.

**What does the Growth Package cost in instalments?**
Three monthly payments of $330, so $990 in total, compared with $950 when paid upfront.

## Changing or stopping

**Can I switch from a Single Strategy Session to a retainer?**
Yes. You do not need to fill in the intake questionnaire again.

**Can I get my money back for the Growth Package?**
Within 14 days of purchase, if you have not used any session, you get a full refund. After that, you get a
pro-rata refund for the unused sessions minus a $50 admin fee.

**How do I cancel the Monthly Retainer?**
After the minimum term of 3 months, cancel with 30 days notice by phone or email. The current month is not
refunded.

**How long does a refund take?**
Elena processes refunds within 2 business days. A card refund usually appears on your statement within
3 to 7 business days.

## Other questions

**Do you give tax or legal advice?**
No. Elena does not provide legal, tax or accounting advice, but she can tell you which questions to ask a
specialist.

**Will you sign an NDA?**
Yes, a standard NDA can be signed before the first working session at no extra cost.

**Do you record our video sessions?**
No, unless both sides agree at the start of the session.
