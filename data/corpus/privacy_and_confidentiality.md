# Ward Advisory — Privacy and Confidentiality

## Confidentiality

Everything you share with Ward Advisory — your numbers, plans, customers and suppliers — is treated as
confidential. It is not shared with other clients or third parties. Elena works with many businesses in
the same area and industries; she never names one client to another without permission.

If your company needs a signed non-disclosure agreement (NDA), Elena signs a standard NDA before the
first working session at no extra cost.

## Calls and recordings

Calls with Ward Advisory may be transcribed and summarised to keep accurate notes of what was agreed.
Before any data is processed, payment card numbers, national ID numbers and email addresses are masked.
Video sessions are not recorded unless both sides agree at the start of the session.

## What data is kept

- Contact details (name, business name, email, phone number).
- Session notes, written summaries and action plans.
- Invoices and payment records (but never full card numbers: card payments go through the secure
  payment link).

## How long data is kept

- Session notes and documents: 2 years after the last session, then deleted.
- Invoices and payment records: as long as the law requires for accounting purposes.
- You can ask for a copy of your session notes at any time.
- You can ask for your session notes to be deleted earlier; this is done within 30 days. Invoices are kept
  even then, because they are required for accounting.

## Marketing and follow-up

Ward Advisory does not send newsletters. Prospects who asked for information may receive one follow-up
email or call. If you do not want to be contacted again, say so by phone or email and you are removed
from the follow-up list on the same day.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Elena Ward directly by phone or email during working hours
(Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm).
