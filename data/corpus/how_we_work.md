# Ward Advisory — How We Work

This document explains what happens from the first contact to the end of a project.

## Working hours

Elena works Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm. Sessions are scheduled within these hours. Calls and emails
outside working hours are answered on the next business day. Ward Advisory is closed on public holidays.

## Step 1: Discovery Call

Every new client starts with a free 20-minute Discovery Call. You can book it by phone or by email.
At the end of the call, Elena recommends a service. You do not have to decide during the call: many
clients ask for a written overview of the services by email and decide later.

## Step 2: Intake questionnaire

Within 24 hours of the Discovery Call (or of booking a service), you receive the intake questionnaire by
email. It asks about your business, your customers, your current numbers and what you want to achieve.
It takes about 30 minutes to complete. Please return it at least 2 business days before your first
session, so Elena has time to prepare.

## Step 3: Agreement and invoice

For the Growth Package and the Monthly Retainer you receive a short agreement and the first invoice.
The work starts once the agreement is signed; the first invoice is due within 14 days, like every
other invoice (see Payment and Invoicing).

## Step 4: Kickoff session

The kickoff session takes place within 10 days of signing the agreement. In this session you agree on
the goals, the priorities and the way you will measure progress.

## Step 5: Written action plan

Within 5 business days after the kickoff session you receive a written action plan. It lists the goals,
the milestones, the topics of the next sessions and the tasks that are on your side.

## Step 6: Working sessions

- Sessions take place mostly online by video call. In-person meetings are possible for clients in the
  local area, at no extra cost.
- You receive a calendar invite for every session. Rescheduling is free if you ask at least 48 hours
  before the session (see the Cancellation and Refund Policy).
- After each Growth Package or retainer session you receive a short email with the agreed next steps,
  usually on the same day.

## Written deliverables at a glance

| Deliverable | When |
|---|---|
| Intake questionnaire (sent to you) | Within 24 hours of the Discovery Call or booking |
| Summary of a Single Strategy Session | Within 2 business days after the session |
| Written action plan (Growth Package, Retainer) | Within 5 business days after the kickoff session |
| Next-steps email after a session | Usually the same day |

If a written deliverable is late, please contact Elena. If the delay is on Ward Advisory's side, Elena
may offer a free 30-minute follow-up call to go through the document together.

## Complaints

If you are not happy with a session or a deliverable, call or email Elena directly. Complaints are
answered within 1 business day. Elena will explain what went wrong and propose a fix — for example
resending a document, correcting an invoice or adding a free follow-up call.

## Ending the collaboration

A Single Strategy Session ends with the written summary. A Growth Package ends after the sixth session.
At the end of a package Elena sends a short closing summary with the results and suggestions for the
next steps. You can always come back later; returning clients do not need a new Discovery Call.
